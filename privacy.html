Winter Arc

Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 September 2026
Winter Arc collects nothing. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code. It cannot send data anywhere, because it has no networking of any kind. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, receive, transmit, sell or share any personal data. There is no server, no account to create and no way to sign in. The developer has no access to anything you enter into the app.

What the app stores on your device

All of this lives in the app's own private storage on your iPhone, and none of it leaves the device:

  • Your arc — its start and end dates, pillars, habits, daily check-ins and reflections.
  • Your "why" statement and your app settings, including the daily reminder time.
  • Before and after photos, if you choose to add them, stored as files in the app's own container.
  • A small summary file that the home-screen widget reads to display your daily counts.

If you use iCloud Backup or an encrypted local backup, this data is included in that backup under Apple's terms. Winter Arc itself does not sync anything.

Camera and photos

  • Camera — used only if you choose to take a before or after photo. Those images are saved inside the app and are never uploaded.
  • Photo library — the app requests add-only access, used solely when you tap to save a before/after comparison image to your own library. The app cannot read, browse or access any existing photos in your library.

Both permissions are optional. Declining either one leaves the rest of the app fully usable.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the notification is scheduled locally by your device. There are no push notifications and no remote server involved. You can turn the reminder off at any time in the app, or in iOS Settings.

Tracking

Winter Arc does not track you. There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no advertising identifier and no third-party framework of any kind in the app. The App Privacy section of our App Store listing reports "Data Not Collected", and the app ships a privacy manifest declaring no tracking and no collected data types.

Children

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Deleting your data

You are always in control of it:

  • Open Settings → Reset app data in the app to permanently erase your arc, check-ins, reflections, photos and preferences.
  • Deleting the app from your iPhone removes all of its data with it.

Since nothing is ever sent to us, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf and no data request to make.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date above. Any change that affects how the app handles your data will also be described in the app's release notes.
