Common questions

What are the three rulesets, and which should I pick?

Streaks counts consecutive days you completed every tracked habit. Simple and strict — one missed day resets the streak.

Consistency Score measures the percentage of habit-days you completed against a target you choose. Forgiving of the occasional miss, good if you want a realistic average rather than a perfect run.

Grace Days gives you a fixed budget of missed days. With a budget of three, three missed days are forgiven; the fourth ends the arc. Good if you want a hard consequence without it being all-or-nothing.

I ran out of grace days. Is the arc over?

You choose. When the budget is exhausted the app shows you the options: Keep going anyway continues tracking with the arc marked failed, so you still finish the ninety days and keep your history. Restart with the same setup archives the current arc and starts a fresh one with your existing pillars and habits pre-filled.

Can I change my habits or pillars in the middle of an arc?

Yes — Settings → Edit habits. A habit you add starts tracking from today, and a habit you remove stops being tracked but stays in your history, so your past days are never rewritten.

Can I check off a day I forgot?

Yes, within the edit window. Open the day from the Progress heat map and toggle it there. Days outside the window are locked and show a caption explaining why, so that your history stays honest.

Why did my day roll over while the app was open?

That is expected. The app watches for midnight and finalises the previous day as soon as it arrives, so your counts are always for the correct date, even if you leave the app open overnight or travel across time zones.

The widget is showing yesterday's numbers.

The widget updates shortly after you toggle a habit, but iOS decides when to refresh it and can delay that to save battery. Opening the app forces it to refresh. If it stays stale, remove the widget and add it again.

Where are my before and after photos stored?

Inside the app's own private storage on your iPhone. They are never uploaded. If you tap to save a comparison to your photo library, the app asks for add-only permission and writes a copy there — it cannot read the rest of your library.

Is my data backed up? What if I get a new phone?

Winter Arc does not sync, but its data is included in your iPhone backup. If you restore a new phone from an iCloud or encrypted local backup, your arc comes with it. Setting up a new phone as new, without restoring a backup, starts you fresh.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Reset app data permanently erases your arc, check-ins, reflections, photos and preferences. Deleting the app removes all of it too.

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. Winter Arc has no networking at all and works identically in airplane mode.

Accessibility

Winter Arc supports VoiceOver, Dynamic Type through the largest accessibility sizes, Reduce Motion and Increase Contrast. If something is hard to use with an accessibility feature turned on, please email me — that is a bug worth fixing.

Reporting a problem

  • What happened, and what you expected instead.
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • The ruleset you are using, and roughly which day of the arc you are on.
  • A screenshot, if the problem is something you can see.
